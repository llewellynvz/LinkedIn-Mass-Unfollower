LinkedIn Mass Unfollower

Automatically unfollow everyone in your Following or Followers list.

  1. Pick a section below — we'll open the right LinkedIn page.
  2. A panel appears top-right with a live count and a Stop button.
  3. Keep the tab open. It pauses automatically if LinkedIn shows a security check.
